# Orbii

> Orbii is a Riyadh-based AI credit-infrastructure company. Its region-specific REST APIs
> (KSA, UAE, Oman) ingest and enrich transaction and invoice data to produce borrower
> intelligence — KPIs, predictive KPIs, risk assessments, band classifications, and
> invoice-based suggested loans — so banks, fintechs and platforms can launch SME lending
> without building an internal credit function. Backed by a $3.6M Prosus-led seed round.

## APIs
- [Orbii API (UAE)](https://api.docs.orbii.ai/uae/docs/orbii-api-v-1-0-5-oas3): base https://api.orbii.ai — 60 operations
- [Orbii KSA API](https://api.docs.orbii.ai/ksa/docs/orbii-ksa-api-v-1-0-3): base https://api.sa.orbii.ai — 49 operations
- [Orbii OMN API](https://api.docs.orbii.ai/omn/docs/orbii-omn-api-v-1-0-3): base https://api.om.orbii.ai — 49 operations
